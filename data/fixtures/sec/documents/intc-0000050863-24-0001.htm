Item 1. Business

Intel Corporation (INTC) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: Intel. Reported segments include: Client Computing (40% of revenue); Data Center & AI (30% of revenue); Intel Foundry (15% of revenue); Other (15% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is idm. We design and manufacture semiconductors in captive fabrication facilities and also describe foundry services initiatives.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other semiconductor designers and manufacturers, including AMD, NVIDIA, and Intel in compute and related markets. We do not compete with semiconductor equipment suppliers as peers in product markets.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Client Computing (40% of revenue); Data Center & AI (30% of revenue); Intel Foundry (15% of revenue); Other (15% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
