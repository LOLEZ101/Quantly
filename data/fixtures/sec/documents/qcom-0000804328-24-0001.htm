Item 1. Business

QUALCOMM Incorporated (QCOM) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: Qualcomm. Reported segments include: QCT (chips) (80% of revenue); QTL (licensing) (20% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is fabless. We design semiconductors and outsource manufacturing to foundries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other companies in our industry.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: QCT (chips) (80% of revenue); QTL (licensing) (20% of revenue).

Geographic Information
