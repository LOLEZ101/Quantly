Item 1. Business

McDonald's Corporation (MCD) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: McDonald's. Reported segments include: Franchised restaurants (60% of revenue); Company-operated restaurants (40% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: Franchise System, Company Operated.

As of year end, approximately 95% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other quick-service and limited-service restaurant brands, including McDonald's, Yum! Brands concepts, Restaurant Brands International brands, Domino's, and fast-casual competitors.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Franchised restaurants (60% of revenue); Company-operated restaurants (40% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
