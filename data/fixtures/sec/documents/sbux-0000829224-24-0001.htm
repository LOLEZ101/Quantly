Item 1. Business

Starbucks Corporation (SBUX) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: Starbucks. Reported segments include: North America (70% of revenue); International (25% of revenue); Channel Development (5% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: Company Operated, Licensed Stores.

As of year end, approximately 45% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other companies in our industry.

Geographic Information
