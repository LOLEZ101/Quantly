Item 1. Business

Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: Micron. Reported segments include: DRAM (70% of revenue); NAND (30% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is memory. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.
