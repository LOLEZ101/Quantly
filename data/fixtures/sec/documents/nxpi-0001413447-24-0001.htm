Item 1. Business

NXP Semiconductors N.V. (NXPI) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: NXP Semiconductors. Reported segments include: Automotive (55% of revenue); Industrial & IoT (30% of revenue); Mobile & Comm Infra (15% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is analog. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.
