Item 1. Business

KLA Corporation (KLAC) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: KLA. Reported segments include: Semiconductor Process Control (90% of revenue); Other (10% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is equipment. We supply wafer fabrication equipment to semiconductor manufacturers.
