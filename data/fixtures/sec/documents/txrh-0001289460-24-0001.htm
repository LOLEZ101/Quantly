Item 1. Business

Texas Roadhouse, Inc. (TXRH) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Principal operations: Texas Roadhouse. Reported segments include: Company restaurants (90% of revenue); Franchise (10% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: Company Operated, Franchise System.

As of year end, approximately 10% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.
