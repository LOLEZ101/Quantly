Item 1. Business

Restaurant Brands International Inc. (QSR) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Restaurant Brands International is an asset-light franchisor of Burger King, Tim Hortons, and Popeyes. System restaurants are predominantly franchised.

Principal operations: Restaurant Brands International Inc.. Reported segments include: Franchise revenues (100% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 94% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Our brands compete with McDonald's, Yum! Brands concepts, and other QSR operators.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Franchise revenues (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
