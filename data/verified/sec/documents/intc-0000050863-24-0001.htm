Item 1. Business

Intel Corporation (INTC) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Intel designs and manufactures semiconductors using an integrated device manufacturing model, including client and data-center processors, and is developing foundry services for external customers.

Principal operations: Intel Corporation. Reported segments include: Client Computing (42% of revenue); Data Center & AI (28% of revenue); Intel Foundry (15% of revenue); Other (15% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is idm. We design and manufacture semiconductors in captive fabrication facilities and also describe foundry services initiatives.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with AMD in CPUs and with other semiconductor companies across product lines. Foundry services compete with dedicated foundries.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Client Computing (42% of revenue); Data Center & AI (28% of revenue); Intel Foundry (15% of revenue); Other (15% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
