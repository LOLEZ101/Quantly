Item 1. Business

American Tower Corporation (AMT) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

American Tower owns and operates wireless and broadcast communications sites and leases space on towers to wireless service providers globally.

Principal operations: American Tower Corporation. Reported segments include: Communications Sites (100% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: communications infrastructure landlord. We lease communications sites to wireless carriers and are not a retail wireless network operator.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Competitors include other communications infrastructure companies such as Crown Castle in the United States.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Communications Sites (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
