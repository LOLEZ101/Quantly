Item 1. Business

Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Broadcom provides semiconductor solutions for networking and infrastructure and also sells infrastructure software. Semiconductor design is primarily fabless.

Principal operations: Broadcom Inc.. Reported segments include: Semiconductor solutions (78% of revenue); Infrastructure software (22% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is fabless. We design semiconductors and outsource manufacturing to foundries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Semiconductor competitors include other networking and infrastructure chip vendors.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Semiconductor solutions (78% of revenue); Infrastructure software (22% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
