Item 1. Business

Texas Roadhouse, Inc. (TXRH) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Texas Roadhouse operates casual dining restaurants that are primarily company-owned, with a smaller franchised mix.

Principal operations: Texas Roadhouse, Inc.. Reported segments include: Company restaurants (92% of revenue); Franchise (8% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 12% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other casual dining steakhouse and full-service restaurants.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Company restaurants (92% of revenue); Franchise (8% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
