Item 1. Business

Monolithic Power Systems, Inc. (MPWR) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Monolithic Power Systems designs power-management and analog semiconductor solutions, primarily using a fabless manufacturing model.

Principal operations: Monolithic Power Systems, Inc.. Reported segments include: Power / Analog (100% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is analog. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other analog and power-management semiconductor companies.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Power / Analog (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
