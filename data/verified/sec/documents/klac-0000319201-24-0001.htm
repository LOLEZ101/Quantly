Item 1. Business

KLA Corporation (KLAC) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

KLA supplies process control and yield-management systems used in semiconductor manufacturing.

Principal operations: KLA Corporation. Reported segments include: Semiconductor Process Control (100% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is equipment. We supply wafer fabrication equipment to semiconductor manufacturers.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other process-control and metrology equipment providers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Semiconductor Process Control (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
