Item 1. Business

Charter Communications, Inc. (CHTR) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Charter provides broadband, video, and voice services under the Spectrum brand over a hybrid fiber-coax network, primarily to residential and SMB customers.

Principal operations: Charter Communications, Inc.. Reported segments include: Internet (57% of revenue); Video / Voice / Mobile (43% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: nationwide wireless network owner.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with telephone companies, fiber overbuilders, and fixed wireless broadband providers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Internet (57% of revenue); Video / Voice / Mobile (43% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
