Item 1. Business

Lam Research Corporation (LRCX) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Lam Research designs and manufactures wafer fabrication equipment focused on etch and deposition processes for semiconductor manufacturing.

Principal operations: Lam Research Corporation. Reported segments include: Systems (68% of revenue); Customer Support (32% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is equipment. We supply wafer fabrication equipment to semiconductor manufacturers.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Competitors include Applied Materials and other wafer-fab equipment suppliers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Systems (68% of revenue); Customer Support (32% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
