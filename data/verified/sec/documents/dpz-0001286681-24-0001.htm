Item 1. Business

Domino's Pizza, Inc. (DPZ) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Domino's franchises and operates pizza delivery and carryout restaurants. The system is heavily franchised and includes supply-chain operations supporting franchisees.

Principal operations: Domino's Pizza, Inc.. Reported segments include: U.S. stores (52% of revenue); International franchise (30% of revenue); Supply chain (18% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 98% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other pizza QSR brands and delivery-oriented restaurants.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: U.S. stores (52% of revenue); International franchise (30% of revenue); Supply chain (18% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
