Item 1. Business

NXP Semiconductors N.V. (NXPI) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

NXP provides mixed-signal and automotive semiconductor solutions. Manufacturing uses a mix of internal and external capacity.

Principal operations: NXP Semiconductors N.V.. Reported segments include: Automotive (56% of revenue); Industrial & IoT (28% of revenue); Mobile & Comm (16% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is analog. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Competitors include other automotive and mixed-signal semiconductor suppliers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Automotive (56% of revenue); Industrial & IoT (28% of revenue); Mobile & Comm (16% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
