Item 1. Business

Verizon Communications Inc. (VZ) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Verizon operates a nationwide wireless network and provides consumer and business connectivity services. Wireless service is the principal driver of consolidated results, with additional wireline and business solutions.

Principal operations: Verizon Communications Inc.. Reported segments include: Wireless (77% of revenue); Business / Wireline (23% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: nationwide wireless network owner.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with AT&T and T-Mobile in U.S. wireless services and with cable operators such as Comcast and Charter for broadband connectivity.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Wireless (77% of revenue); Business / Wireline (23% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
