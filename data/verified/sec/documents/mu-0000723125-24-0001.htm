Item 1. Business

Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Micron designs and manufactures memory and storage products, principally DRAM and NAND.

Principal operations: Micron Technology, Inc.. Reported segments include: DRAM (72% of revenue); NAND (28% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is memory. We design and manufacture memory semiconductors in captive fabrication facilities.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other memory semiconductor manufacturers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: DRAM (72% of revenue); NAND (28% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
