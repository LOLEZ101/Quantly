Item 1. Business

Applied Materials, Inc. (AMAT) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Applied Materials supplies manufacturing equipment and services used to produce semiconductor chips, including deposition and related wafer-fab tools.

Principal operations: Applied Materials, Inc.. Reported segments include: Semiconductor Systems (78% of revenue); Applied Global Services (22% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is equipment. We supply wafer fabrication equipment to semiconductor manufacturers.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other semiconductor equipment companies such as Lam Research and process-control suppliers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Semiconductor Systems (78% of revenue); Applied Global Services (22% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
