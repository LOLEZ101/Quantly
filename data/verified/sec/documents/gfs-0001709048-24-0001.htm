Item 1. Business

GlobalFoundries Inc. (GFS) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

GlobalFoundries is a pure-play semiconductor foundry manufacturing wafers for third-party fabless and IDM customers.

Principal operations: GlobalFoundries Inc.. Reported segments include: Wafer manufacturing (100% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is foundry. We operate as a pure-play foundry manufacturing wafers for third-party designers.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other pure-play and captive foundries for wafer manufacturing services.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Wafer manufacturing (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
