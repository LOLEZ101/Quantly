Item 1. Business

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

NVIDIA designs GPUs and accelerated computing platforms. We are a fabless semiconductor company; wafer manufacturing is performed by foundry partners.

Principal operations: NVIDIA Corporation. Reported segments include: Compute & Networking (82% of revenue); Graphics (18% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is fabless. We design semiconductors and outsource manufacturing to foundries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with AMD and other accelerated computing suppliers. We do not compete with wafer-fab equipment companies as product peers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Compute & Networking (82% of revenue); Graphics (18% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
