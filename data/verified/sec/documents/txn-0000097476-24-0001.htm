Item 1. Business

Texas Instruments Incorporated (TXN) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Texas Instruments designs and manufactures analog and embedded processing semiconductors, with substantial internal manufacturing capacity.

Principal operations: Texas Instruments Incorporated. Reported segments include: Analog (76% of revenue); Embedded Processing (24% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is analog. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other analog and embedded semiconductor suppliers such as Analog Devices.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Analog (76% of revenue); Embedded Processing (24% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
