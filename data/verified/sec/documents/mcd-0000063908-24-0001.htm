Item 1. Business

McDonald's Corporation (MCD) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

McDonald's franchises and operates McDonald's restaurants. The substantial majority of restaurants globally are franchised; franchise royalties and fees are material to operating income.

Principal operations: McDonald's Corporation. Reported segments include: Franchised restaurants (58% of revenue); Company-operated restaurants (42% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 95% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with other global QSR brands including concepts owned by Yum! Brands and Restaurant Brands International, as well as pizza and coffee chains.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Franchised restaurants (58% of revenue); Company-operated restaurants (42% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
