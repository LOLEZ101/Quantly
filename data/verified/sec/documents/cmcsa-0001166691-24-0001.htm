Item 1. Business

Comcast Corporation (CMCSA) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Comcast operates cable communications (broadband, video, voice) and media businesses. Connectivity through the cable platform is a core economic engine.

Principal operations: Comcast Corporation. Reported segments include: Connectivity & Platforms (58% of revenue); Content & Experiences (42% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: nationwide wireless network owner.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Broadband competes with telephone companies and fixed wireless. Media competes with other content platforms.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Connectivity & Platforms (58% of revenue); Content & Experiences (42% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
