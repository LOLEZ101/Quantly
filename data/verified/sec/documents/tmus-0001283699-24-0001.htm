Item 1. Business

T-Mobile US, Inc. (TMUS) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

T-Mobile US operates a nationwide wireless communications network focused primarily on postpaid and prepaid mobile services.

Principal operations: T-Mobile US, Inc.. Reported segments include: Wireless (100% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: nationwide wireless network owner.
