Item 1. Business

Starbucks Corporation (SBUX) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Starbucks operates and licenses Starbucks coffeehouses. Company-operated stores are the primary model in the United States, with licensed stores more common in some international markets.

Principal operations: Starbucks Corporation. Reported segments include: North America (72% of revenue); International (23% of revenue); Channel Development (5% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 48% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with specialty coffee retailers, QSR chains, and convenience beverage outlets.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: North America (72% of revenue); International (23% of revenue); Channel Development (5% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
