Item 1. Business

Analog Devices, Inc. (ADI) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Analog Devices designs and manufactures high-performance analog, mixed-signal, and digital signal processing products.

Principal operations: Analog Devices, Inc.. Reported segments include: Analog / Mixed Signal (100% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is analog. We produce analog and mixed-signal devices.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Competitors include Texas Instruments and other analog/mixed-signal semiconductor companies.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Analog / Mixed Signal (100% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
