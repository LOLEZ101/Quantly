Item 1. Business

QUALCOMM Incorporated (QCOM) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Qualcomm develops and licenses wireless technology and sells fabless mobile and connectivity semiconductor products (QCT) and licensing (QTL).

Principal operations: QUALCOMM Incorporated. Reported segments include: QCT (82% of revenue); QTL (18% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is fabless. We design semiconductors and outsource manufacturing to foundries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Chip competitors include other mobile and connectivity semiconductor companies.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: QCT (82% of revenue); QTL (18% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
