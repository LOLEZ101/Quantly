Item 1. Business

Yum! Brands, Inc. (YUM) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Yum! Brands is an asset-light franchisor of KFC, Taco Bell, and Pizza Hut. Nearly all system restaurants are franchised.

Principal operations: Yum! Brands, Inc.. Reported segments include: Franchise and property revenues (88% of revenue); Company sales (12% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 98% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Brand concepts compete with other QSR operators including McDonald's and Restaurant Brands International brands.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Franchise and property revenues (88% of revenue); Company sales (12% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
