Item 1. Business

Darden Restaurants, Inc. (DRI) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

Darden owns and operates full-service restaurants including Olive Garden and other casual dining brands. Restaurants are primarily company-operated.

Principal operations: Darden Restaurants, Inc.. Reported segments include: Olive Garden (54% of revenue); Other brands (46% of revenue).

As of year end, approximately 0% of system restaurants were franchised and the remainder were company-operated.
