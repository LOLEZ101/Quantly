Item 1. Business

AT&T Inc. (T) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

AT&T provides communications and technology services, including wireless mobility and wireline broadband/fiber services to consumers and businesses in the United States.

Principal operations: AT&T Inc.. Reported segments include: Mobility (64% of revenue); Business Wireline (26% of revenue); Consumer Wireline (10% of revenue).

Our infrastructure/operating model includes: nationwide wireless network owner.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

Primary wireless competitors include Verizon and T-Mobile. Wireline broadband competes with cable and fiber providers.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Mobility (64% of revenue); Business Wireline (26% of revenue); Consumer Wireline (10% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
