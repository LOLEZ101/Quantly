Item 1. Business

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a registrant under the Securities Exchange Act.

AMD designs high-performance CPUs, GPUs, and adaptive computing products as a fabless semiconductor company using external foundries.

Principal operations: Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.. Reported segments include: Data Center (46% of revenue); Client (28% of revenue); Gaming (16% of revenue); Embedded (10% of revenue).

Our semiconductor business model is fabless. We design semiconductors and outsource manufacturing to foundries.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to competitive, regulatory, and technology risks.

Competition

We compete with NVIDIA in GPUs/accelerators and with Intel in CPUs and related processors.

Notes to Financial Statements — Segment Information

Revenue by segment: Data Center (46% of revenue); Client (28% of revenue); Gaming (16% of revenue); Embedded (10% of revenue).

Geographic Information

We generate revenue primarily in the United States and other international markets.
